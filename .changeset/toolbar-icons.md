Rework the toolbar and the status bar. Text labels are replaced by icons, the
toolbar is reduced to previous, next and settings, and the zoom controls move
next to the zoom percentage in the status bar. The status bar can no longer be
hidden, so the `toggle_bottom_bar` action, its `b` shortcut and the matching
setting are gone. Fit, Fit Best, Original Size, Play, Copy and Smooth remain
available through their shortcuts and the context menu.
